Method

On an AMM you don't trade against a person, you trade against a pool. No tool can prove wallet A handed money to wallet B, and anything claiming otherwise is selling certainty it doesn't have.

Fillmark measures overlap. Per pool, buys and sells are netted inside a short window. A wallet reducing supply into the window your position opened is attributed a share of that supply, weighted by size. Every row carries its window, so the attribution is checkable rather than asserted.

Realised profit and loss only — open positions are excluded, because an unrealised loss hasn't paid anyone yet.
