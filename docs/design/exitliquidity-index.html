How this is built

A wallet appears here when it was reducing supply into the same window that somebody else's losing position opened, in the same pool. Its rank is the sum of those attributed dollars over the window.

This is overlap, not proof. On an AMM you trade against a pool, not a person, and nothing here claims otherwise. Every row on an extractor's own file carries the pool, timestamp and slot range it came from, so the attribution can be checked rather than trusted.

  • CountedRealised losses only. An open position hasn't paid anyone yet, so it is excluded.
  • ExcludedThe loser's own wallets and funding cluster; LP adds and removes; CEX hot wallets, routers and aggregator vaults.
  • ExcludedAny wallet whose net position change inside the window is under 20% of its gross volume — that is a market maker round-tripping, not a distributor.
  • WeightingEach sell is weighted by size and by how close it sat to the buy it is being netted against.
  • Cost basisTokens that arrived by transfer rather than by swap have no basis, and those positions are dropped instead of counted as free profit.

A high rank is not an accusation. Market makers, arbitrage desks and ordinary early buyers all appear on this list. What the number says is how much of other people's realised loss overlapped with this address — nothing more.
